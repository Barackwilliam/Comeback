Our Values

Why Choose Sunrise & Sunset

Local Experts, Global Standards
Our guides are Tanzanian nationals with years of experience and official licenses from TANAPA and TATO. We know the parks, the animals’ behavior, and the best hidden spots most tourists miss..
Customized Itineraries
No cookie-cutter packages. We design every trip around your budget, time, interests, and travel style — whether it’s luxury, mid-range, or budget camping.
Well-Maintained Safari Vehicles
All our 4x4 Land Cruisers and Land Rovers are serviced regularly and equipped with pop-up roofs, charging ports, cool boxes, and first aid kits for comfort and safety.
Transparent & Fair Pricing
What you see is what you pay. We include all taxes, park fees, and permits upfront so there are no surprises
24/7 Support
From booking to the last day of your trip, our team is available on WhatsApp and phone to assist you. We’re on the ground, not overseas
Responsible Tourism
We partner with local lodges, employ local staff, and support community projects. A portion of our proceeds goes toward conservation and education initiatives
