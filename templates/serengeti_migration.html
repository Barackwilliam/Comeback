Great Migration

Every year, over 1.5 million wildebeest, 200,000 zebras, and hundreds of thousands of gazelles undertake a perilous circular journey across Tanzania and Kenya in search of fresh grazing — the greatest wildlife migration on Earth.

The dramatic river crossings at Mara and Grumeti rivers — where crocodiles lie in wait — are among the most breathtaking spectacles in the natural world.
