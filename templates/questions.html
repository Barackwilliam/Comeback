We recommend neutral-coloured clothing, a good hat, sunscreen, insect repellent, binoculars, a camera, and comfortable walking shoes. A full packing list will be provided upon booking confirmation.

Yes, Tanzania is generally very safe for tourists. Our team monitors conditions closely and all our safari routes are well-established and regularly used. Your safety is always our top priority.

The best time for wildlife viewing is June to October (dry season). The Great Migration river crossings peak in July to September. However, Tanzania offers incredible experiences year-round. Contact us to plan the ideal time for your interests.

Most nationalities require a visa to enter Tanzania. Tanzania now offers an eVisa which can be applied for online before your trip. We recommend applying at least 2 weeks in advance. We can guide you through the process upon booking.

Game drives typically last 3–6 hours, either in the morning or afternoon. Full-day drives with a packed lunch are also available. The duration depends on your itinerary and the wildlife activity on the day.
